# Dataset attribution and licensing

`quotes.json` contains an extraction from English Wikiquote, contributed by
Wikiquote editors. Each record identifies the article, permanent revision,
and article history for contributor attribution.

Wikiquote contributor text is licensed under Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0, unless otherwise noted:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Underlying quotations may be separately copyrighted and included under fair
use. A Wikiquote page's license does not automatically license every quotation
on that page. Consult the original source and Wikiquote's copyright policy:
https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Wikiquote:Copyrights

Changes made in this dataset: HTML removed; quote text, citation, source links,
year evidence, author, and revision attribution extracted; ambiguous or undated
records omitted. Shareable adaptations of the Wikiquote contributor material
retain the applicable share-alike license. The repository's MIT code license
does not override the rights in the dataset's source material.
